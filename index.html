BUILDING MATERIALS.

Through our in-house building material supply division, we provide essential construction materials with dependable sourcing and timely availability across North Goa.

GOA SPECIALTY

Laterite Stones

Iconic hand-cut and machine-cut red laterite masonry blocks sourced directly from premium Goan stone quarries.

FINE GRADE

Natural Sand

Clean, double-washed river sand ideal for structural RCC concreting, brickwork masonry, and fine plastering.

GRANITE BLUE METAL

Aggregates

Crushed granite stone aggregates available in standard 10mm, 20mm, and 40mm sizes for high-strength concrete mixes.

HEAVY FOUNDATION

Rubble Stones

Uncut quarry hard rocks for soling, foundation footings, compound walls, and landslide retaining structures.

PLASTER MIX

Powder Mix Khadi

Finely crushed stone dust and khadi powder for road bedding, paving substrate, and smooth wall rendering.

BRANDED CEMENT

Cement

Direct dealer supply of Grade 53 / 43 OPC and PPC cement from top brands (UltraTech, ACC, Ambuja).

HIGH TENSILE

Steel (TMT Bars)

Fe 550 & Fe 500D corrosion-resistant TMT rebar reinforcement for earthquake-safe Goan structures.

LIGHTWEIGHT

AAC Blocks

Autoclaved aerated concrete blocks offering thermal insulation, rapid construction speed, and lightweight wall loads.
